Things to Do in Middletown

From river views to rugged trails, Middletown has plenty to fill a morning, an afternoon, or a whole weekend.

Parks & trails

Parks and trails

Here are the places locals point visitors toward first.

Parks & trails

Harbor Park

Harbor Park offers some of the best views of the Connecticut River in the city — an easy, relaxing stop for a walk or a quiet moment by the water.

Parks & trails

Seven Falls Park

For something more active, Seven Falls Park features rugged trails and beautiful waterfalls. It's a favourite among hikers and anyone who likes their nature with a little bit of a climb.

Parks & trails

Higby Mountain Park

Higby Mountain Park is a popular spot for trails and picnicking, making it a good choice for a half-day outing with family or friends.

Historic places

Landmarks

Landmark

Wadsworth Mansion

The Wadsworth Mansion is a historic mansion in Middletown that hosts events — a landmark worth knowing about whether you're planning a visit or a celebration.

Practicalities

Before you go

Trail surfaces at Seven Falls Park are rugged, so plan footwear and timing accordingly. For parking, restrooms, and current seasonal scheduling at any city park, check with the city before you set out — the News & Events page carries current notices.
