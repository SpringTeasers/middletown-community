Accessibility

Accessibility statement

A public civic site is used by everyone — older residents, screen-reader users, people on a phone in bright sun, people reading in a second language. Accessibility here is a functional requirement, not a nicety.

The standard we target

WCAG 2.2 Level AA

This site is built and tested against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, with Level AAA body-text contrast wherever the colour palette allows.

Conformance is a target and an ongoing process, not a one-off badge. The measures below are built into every page of this site.

If a part of the site doesn't work for you, that is a problem worth fixing — report it using the contact page and choose "Accessibility problem" as the subject.

At a glance

  • Target: WCAG 2.2 Level AA
  • Keyboard-operable throughout, with a visible focus ring
  • Light and dark themes, following your system preference
  • Reduced-motion preference respected

How the site is built

Measures in place

Colour and contrast

Text meets at least 4.5:1 contrast, and large text at least 3:1. Meaning is never carried by colour alone — status labels, errors and the current page all have text or shape as well as hue.

Keyboard and focus

Every interactive element is reachable and operable by keyboard, in an order that matches the page. A visible focus ring is shown throughout, and the mobile menu closes with Escape and returns focus to the button that opened it.

Structure and headings

Each page has one main heading and one main landmark, navigation regions are labelled, breadcrumbs show your position, and lists and tables use real list and table markup rather than layouts of divs.

Images and text

Decorative images are hidden from screen readers; meaningful ones carry descriptive alternative text. Essential information is written as real text, never baked into an image.

Forms

Every field has a visible label. Errors are described in words, marked on the field, and summarised at the top of the form, with focus moved to the first problem. There is no CAPTCHA or other cognitive test.

Motion, zoom and resizing

The site works at 320 pixels wide and at 400% zoom without horizontal scrolling. Animations and smooth scrolling are switched off when your system asks for reduced motion, and nothing flashes or auto-advances.

Honest limitations

Known constraints

Please read

This is a community information site, not the official City of Middletown website. It links out to city and third-party pages — for example the government directory, the library, and transit information — whose accessibility is outside our control.

The contact form on the contact page is a front-end demonstration in this build and has no mail server attached; the official contact options are listed alongside it and remain the reliable route.

Where a fact about the city changes — a phone number, an address, opening hours — it can go stale between updates. Each page notes its sources so anything important can be checked at the official page.
